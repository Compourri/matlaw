Conveyancing Attorneys You Can Trust

When buying, selling, or transferring property in South Africa, you need experienced conveyancing attorneys by your side. Matthee Attorneys Inc provides professional, reliable, and fully compliant conveyancing services to clients across Fochville and Gauteng.

We guide you through every step of the property transfer process, ensuring your transaction is secure, legally binding, and completed efficiently. Our team delivers practical solutions, clear communication, and meticulous attention to detail.

Comprehensive Conveyancing Services

We offer a full range of conveyancing services to meet all your property transfer needs in Fochville.

Property Transfers

Buying or selling property involves complex legal processes. We handle every aspect, from contract review to registration at the Deeds Office.

  • Preparation and review of sale agreements
  • Property transfer registration with the Deeds Office
  • Liaising with banks and estate agents for bond registration

Learn more about our Notary Services.

Bond Registration and Financing

If your property purchase involves a mortgage, we manage the bond registration process efficiently and liaise directly with lenders.

  • Drafting and reviewing bond documents
  • Submitting required documents to banks
  • Coordinating with the Deeds Office for registration

For official guidance, visit the South African Deeds Office.

Property Law Advice

Property transactions require a thorough understanding of South African property law. We provide expert advice on contracts, property rights, and potential risks.

  • Legal compliance for property transfers
  • Resolving disputes and queries
  • Guidance on complex property transactions

For additional resources, visit the Law Society of South Africa.

Deceased Estate Property Transfers

Transferring property from a deceased estate requires careful handling. We ensure legal compliance and smooth transfers for heirs and beneficiaries.

  • Deceased estate property registration
  • Liaising with the Master of the High Court
  • Accurate distribution and documentation
