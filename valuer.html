Associated Valuer – Trusted Property Valuations

Reliable property valuations are essential for informed decisions. At Matthee Attorneys Inc, we work with experienced, qualified valuers to provide independent, legally compliant reports for estate planning, conveyancing, sales, and financial transactions.

Whether you are buying or selling property, settling an estate, refinancing, or resolving a dispute, accurate valuations are crucial. We ensure every report is thorough, precise, and accepted for legal and financial purposes.

Our Valuation Services

Property Transfers and Sales

Accurate valuations help buyers and sellers make informed decisions. We ensure fair and transparent property transfers.

  • Market value assessment
  • Sale agreement review support
  • Registration support for conveyancing

For seamless property transfers, see our conveyancing services.

Estate and Probate Valuations

Property valuations are required for deceased estate administration. We provide independent reports accepted by the Master of the High Court for smooth estate administration and legal compliance.

For official guidance, visit the Master of the High Court.

Bond and Mortgage Valuations

Lenders require professional valuations before approving bonds. Our reports satisfy banks and financial institutions for fast loan processing.

Learn more about property transactions via our conveyancing services.

Legal and Dispute Support

In property disputes, independent valuations provide clarity and legal weight. Our reports support legal proceedings, including family law, estate disputes, and commercial matters.

Why Choose Us?

  • Clear explanations and practical advice
  • Reports for conveyancing, estate, refinancing, or disputes
