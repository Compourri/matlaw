Matthee Attorneys History

For over 50 years, Matthee Attorneys has served the Fochville community since our establishment in 1971. What began as a small practice has grown into a highly respected legal firm, renowned for our professionalism, integrity, and client-focused approach. Throughout the decades, we have maintained an unwavering commitment to excellence while ensuring full compliance with South African legislation, including POPIA and PAIA.

Founding and Early Years

Founded in 1971, Matthee Attorneys began as a small local practice specializing in general legal matters. From the very beginning, we emphasized ethical service and personal attention to our clients. This approach built trust and established a solid foundation for long-term growth. Our early achievements in civil, commercial, and property-related matters quickly earned us a reputation for reliability and professionalism.

Expansion and Growth

The 1980s and 1990s marked a period of significant expansion. We broadened our services to include conveyancing, estate administration, and notarial work. During this time, we invested heavily in staff development, modern office systems, and improved client service protocols. By the early 2000s, Matthee Attorneys had established itself as a leading local legal practice, serving private clients, corporations, and financial institutions alike.

Key Milestones

  • 1971 - Matthee Attorneys established in Fochville
  • 1980s - Expanded into conveyancing and estate administration
  • 1990s - Added notarial and commercial legal services
  • 2000s - Implemented advanced client communication and record management systems
  • 2010s - Recognized for excellence in legal services and community engagement
  • 2020s - Continued digital transformation while maintaining regulatory compliance

Legal Excellence and Compliance

We ensure that all our services comply fully with South African law. Our firm adheres strictly to POPIA, PAIA, and all other applicable legislation. For detailed compliance information, please refer to our PAIA Manual and Privacy Policy. This dedication to legal compliance reflects our commitment to accountability and professional excellence.

Community Engagement and Legacy

Matthee Attorneys has remained actively involved in our community through pro bono work, educational initiatives, and civic participation. This ongoing commitment strengthens our legacy as a trusted legal institution. Combined with our five decades of achievements, these contributions continue to foster confidence and lasting relationships with our clients and partners.

Looking Forward

With over 50 years of experience, Matthee Attorneys remains steadfast in our pursuit of excellence, integrity, and innovation. We continue to evolve with the changing legal landscape, embracing new technologies and processes to better serve our clients. Our rich history ensures that we are well-positioned to provide professional, reliable, and compliant legal services for generations to come.
