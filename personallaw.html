Personal Law Attorneys You Can Trust

When legal matters affect your family, assets, and long-term security, you need experienced personal law attorneys. At Matthee Attorneys Inc, we provide professional, compassionate, and legally sound assistance to individuals and families seeking clarity and certainty.

Whether you need help with wills, trusts, estate planning, antenuptial contracts, or deceased estates, our team is committed to protecting what matters most to you.

Comprehensive Personal Law Services

We offer a full range of personal law services to meet your family and estate planning needs in Fochville.

Wills and Estate Planning

Proper estate planning ensures your wishes are honoured and your loved ones are protected. We draft legally valid wills that reduce uncertainty, prevent disputes, and simplify estate administration.

  • Drafting and reviewing wills
  • Estate structuring and beneficiary planning
  • Updating wills to reflect life changes

Estate planning often involves property. Learn more about our conveyancing services.

Trusts and Fiduciary Advice

Trusts are valuable tools for asset protection and long-term planning. We guide you through establishing and administering trusts in compliance with South African fiduciary law.

  • Family and inter vivos trusts
  • Testamentary trusts
  • Legal compliance and trustee duties

For official information, visit the Master of the High Court.

Antenuptial Contracts

Marriage has legal and financial consequences. We draft antenuptial contracts that clearly define matrimonial property regimes and protect both parties' interests.

  • Antenuptial contracts with accrual
  • Antenuptial contracts without accrual
  • Registration and statutory compliance

Where matrimonial planning involves property, our conveyancing team can assist.

Deceased Estates Administration

Administering a deceased estate can be complex and emotionally demanding. We handle the full process with professionalism and care.

  • Reporting estates to the Master
  • Managing estate assets and liabilities
  • Distributing inheritances to beneficiaries

For legal standards and professional guidance, visit the Law Society of South Africa.
