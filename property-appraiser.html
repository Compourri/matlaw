Expert property appraisal services

EXPERT PROPERTY APPRAISER – RELIABLE VALUATIONS

Accurate, independent, and legally compliant property valuations are essential. At Matthee Attorneys Inc., our expert appraisers provide thorough assessments for sales, refinancing, estate planning, and legal purposes.

Whether you are buying, selling, or refinancing property, or need a valuation for estate or legal matters, our expert appraisals ensure accurate property values. We combine market knowledge, legal compliance, and professional integrity.

Our appraisers are registered with the South African Council for the Property Valuers Profession (SACPVP) and members of the South African Institute of Valuers (SAIV), ensuring all reports meet the required regulatory standards.

OUR APPRAISAL SERVICES

SALES AND MARKET VALUATIONS

Accurate valuations are vital for fair property sales. We provide independent reports that reflect current market conditions, helping buyers and sellers make informed decisions.

  • Residential and commercial valuations
  • Market value assessments
  • Sale agreement and negotiation support

For a seamless property transfer, see our conveyancing services.

REFINANCING AND BOND VALUATIONS

Banks require professional appraisals before approving mortgages or refinancing. Our appraisers prepare reports that satisfy lenders for smooth financing.

  • Mortgage and bond valuations
  • Independent reports for banks
  • Property finance compliance documentation

For South African valuation standards, visit the South African Property Valuers Institute (SAPV).

ESTATE AND PROBATE VALUATIONS

Property valuations are required for estate administration and probate. We provide accurate, independent reports accepted by the Master of the High Court.

  • Estate property assessments
  • Reports for deceased estate administration
  • Master of the High Court compliance

For official guidance, visit the Master of the High Court.

LEGAL AND DISPUTE VALUATIONS

In property disputes, expert valuations provide essential evidence. Our appraisals support court proceedings, mediations, and settlements.

  • Expert witness reports
  • Mediation and dispute resolution support
  • Family law and divorce property divisions
