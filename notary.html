Notary Services You Can Trust

Notary services are essential for certifying documents, authenticating contracts, and ensuring legal compliance. At Matthee Attorneys Inc, our experienced notaries provide reliable, professional services for individuals and businesses in Fochville and across Gauteng.

We handle contracts, affidavits, powers of attorney, property documents, and estate-related documentation. With attention to detail and full legal compliance, we make the process straightforward.

Our Comprehensive Notary Services

Document Certification and Authentication

We certify and authenticate documents to ensure they are legally valid and recognised by authorities. Whether for business, property, or personal matters, our notaries handle each case with precision.

  • Certification of copies
  • Verification of signatures
  • Document authentication

For related services, see our Personal Law page.

Powers of Attorney

A power of attorney grants someone legal authority to act on your behalf. We draft, notarise, and register powers of attorney that are legally binding and fully compliant.

  • General powers of attorney
  • Special powers for property or financial matters
  • Advice on revocation and amendments

For official requirements, visit the Department of Justice.

Property and Conveyancing Documents

Notarisation is often required in property transactions. Our notary services complement our conveyancing expertise.

  • Deeds, contracts, and property agreements
  • Notarising affidavits and declarations
  • Liaising with the Deeds Office

Learn more about our conveyancing services.

Wills, Trusts, and Estate Documents

We also handle estate-related documentation. Notarising these documents ensures legal recognition and reduces potential disputes.

  • Notarising wills and testamentary documents
  • Trust agreements and fiduciary documents
  • Affidavits for deceased estates

For official guidelines, visit the Master of the High Court.
